# Product

## What it is
Moondune ("Desert Alchemy — Redefined") is a climate-intelligent skincare brand from Masdar City, Abu Dhabi (House of Asya Limited, founder Sruti Gudavalli). All-in-one AM and PM essences pair Arabic botanicals (dates, coffee, honey) with Korean bio-science (fermented extracts, ceramides, beta-glucan, multi-molecular hyaluronic acid), made and lab-tested in South Korea.

## Who it's for
UAE residents (expats and locals, unisex) whose skin struggles with 45°C heat, humidity and all-day air-conditioning; people who want a short routine (one step AM, one step PM).

## Proof (verified — never inflate)
- Finalist, Innovative Packaging — Beautyworld Middle East Awards 2026 (a *finalist*, not a winner).
- Press: BeautyMatter, Gulf Times Now; upcoming Falcons of Majlis; Ahaana Khosla reel.
- 7 independent Korean lab reports (stability incl. high heat, skin irritation, challenge, compatibility, microbiology, non-nano, no animal testing).
- Free tracked 2-day UAE delivery (Aramex), VAT & duties included, cash on delivery, Stripe card payments.

## Surfaces & modes
- Storefront home, shop, product pages: **Persuade** — the add-to-cart action lives in the first viewport.
- Account, checkout: **Operate**.
- FAQs, policies, ingredients: **Read**.

## Voice
Warm, confident, specific to the region; no hype words. Short sentences, real numbers (45°C, 2-day, 1200ml).
